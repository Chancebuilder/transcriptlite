Cookie & Device-Storage Disclosure

Effective October 4, 2026

Current TranscriptLite configuration: coursework may be retained using browser local storage so your reviewed courses remain on this device. TranscriptLite does not currently require advertising or behavioral-tracking cookies to evaluate a transcript.

Local storage

Browser local storage may hold reviewed coursework and application preferences. This information remains available until the application or browser storage is cleared. Anyone with access to the same browser profile may potentially access locally retained information.

Hosting and essential technology

The hosting platform may process technical request data needed to deliver and secure the site. If analytics, advertising, authentication, or other non-essential technologies are added, this disclosure and any required consent controls should be updated before activation.

Your control

You can clear local storage through TranscriptLite where controls are provided or through your browser settings. Clearing it can remove saved coursework and preferences from that browser.
