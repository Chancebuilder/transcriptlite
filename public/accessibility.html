Accessibility Statement

Effective October 4, 2026

The Degree Agency is committed to making TranscriptLite usable by as many people as reasonably possible, including people using assistive technologies.

Approach

We aim to use semantic interfaces, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, labeled form controls, responsive layouts, readable contrast, and generally recognized accessibility practices, with WCAG used as a reference point.

Known limitations

Accessibility can be affected by browsers, devices, uploaded PDF structure, and future third-party services. PDF extraction quality can also vary by source document.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, contact The Degree Agency through its published contact channel and identify the page, control, or content involved. We will make reasonable efforts to provide an alternative method.
