Educational Planning & Transcript Disclaimer

Effective October 4, 2026

TranscriptLite is a planning product of The Degree Agency. It is not an official transcript evaluation, admissions decision, transfer-credit award, degree audit, accreditation determination, financial-aid determination, or promise of graduation.

Course classifications

Labels such as Humanities, Natural Sciences, Social & Behavioral Sciences, Business, Computer Science, Cybersecurity, Data Science, and similar categories may be automated best guesses. They describe planning classifications, not an institution's final classification.

Degree applicability

A recommendation that coursework may satisfy General Education, Major Core, Major Elective, Supporting/Related, or Free Elective requirements is provisional unless supported by documented institution-specific evidence. The receiving institution has final authority.

Transferability

Credit recommendations, institutional acceptance, course equivalencies, and degree applicability are different facts. Evidence of one must not be treated as proof of another.

Results vary

Policies and individual academic histories vary. Users should obtain an official evaluation from the receiving institution before relying on TranscriptLite for enrollment, withdrawal, payment, graduation, licensure, or other material decisions.
