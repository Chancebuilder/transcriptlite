TranscriptLite Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

Local-first transcript processing. In the current TranscriptLite configuration, transcript PDFs are processed in your browser rather than uploaded to The Degree Agency. Reviewed coursework may be stored in your browser's local storage on your device. Public institution and program reference searches may be sent to the Education Data Core.

Information processed

TranscriptLite may process coursework, institution names, course codes, titles, credits, grades, terms, degree interests, receiving-school selections, and related planning information you enter or import. The service may also receive ordinary technical request information from its hosting provider.

How information is used

Information is used to organize coursework, classify courses, model potential degree applicability, retrieve public education-reference data, operate and secure the service, and provide requested planning features.

Academic records and FERPA

The Degree Agency is an independent education consulting company, not a school or registrar. Information submitted directly to an independent consultant is not automatically a FERPA education record. If a covered educational institution formally engages The Degree Agency, the applicable agreement and legal requirements control.

AI and recommendations

Automated or heuristic classifications and recommendations may be incomplete or wrong. Identifiable transcripts are not intended to be used to train public AI models. Institution-specific evidence and official school decisions override planning recommendations.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to host, secure, or operate the service, when directed by you, or when legally required. TranscriptLite does not intentionally send transcript content to advertising platforms.

Retention and control

Browser-local coursework remains on the device until you clear it through the application or browser storage. Server-side technical records may be retained as reasonably necessary for security and operations. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, restrict, object to, or withdraw consent for covered processing.

Security

We use data minimization and local-first processing where practical, but no system is perfectly secure. Do not provide Social Security numbers, passwords, banking credentials, full payment-card data, or unrelated sensitive records.

Adults and changes

The Degree Agency's services are intended for adults 18 and older. This notice may be updated as TranscriptLite adds accounts, cloud storage, AI providers, analytics, or other features.
